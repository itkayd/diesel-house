Store mock-up: how to read it

Tap the gold bag (top right) or the gem chip. Try buying characters with coins or gems. Run out of gems to see the "get more" path.

  • Catches chip 🐾 7/15 (40 with VIP), resets at your local midnight.
  • VIP Pass (Gems tab): 3/7/30 days for gems, never auto-renews, buying again adds time (max 180 days). No subscription.
  • Wallet chips sit under the zone pill, next to the XP chip. Coins have no "+" (they can't be bought); gems have "+" and open the Gems tab.
  • Each item has one price, in coins or gems.
  • The confirm sheet shows the maths: balance now, minus the price, gives the balance after. Without enough funds, the button turns into the cheapest pack that covers the gap.
  • Preview moves: walk, attack and jump (Ninja Adventure sheets), so people can see the "crazy moves" before buying.
  • Buying disables the button and sends an idempotency_key, so a double tap can never charge twice (STORE.md §5.2).
  • Here the wallet is local, for the mock only. In the game, the server's answer is the only truth.
